# Retail_Business_Performance_Analysis
## 📊 Retail Business Performance & Profitability Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes the Superstore retail dataset to understand sales performance, profitability, product categories, sub-categories, regional performance, and the impact of discounts.

The project uses Python, SQL, and Power BI to identify profitable business areas, loss-making segments, and opportunities to improve overall profitability.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze overall sales and profit.
- Compare performance across product categories.
- Identify profitable and loss-making sub-categories.
- Analyze regional sales and profitability.
- Study the relationship between discounts and profit.
- Create an interactive Power BI dashboard.
- Provide business recommendations based on the analysis.

## 🗂️ Dataset

The project uses the Superstore retail dataset.

- Records: 10,194
- Columns: 21
- Main fields: Order Date, Customer, Category, Sub-Category, Region, Sales, Quantity, Discount, and Profit.

## 💡 Key Findings

- Total sales were approximately $2.33 million.
- Total profit was approximately $292.30 thousand.
- Technology was the strongest-performing category.
- The West region generated the highest profit.
- Copiers were the most profitable sub-category.
- Tables, Bookcases, and Supplies generated losses.
- Higher discount levels were associated with lower profitability.
- Furniture generated strong sales but comparatively low profit.

## 📌 Recommendations

- Reduce discounts above 20%.
- Review pricing and costs for loss-making sub-categories.
- Investigate loss-making states and regions.
- Focus marketing efforts on profitable Technology products.
- Study the successful performance of the West region.
- Improve pricing and discount strategies to increase profit margins.
